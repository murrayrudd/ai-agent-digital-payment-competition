# Data provenance and units

Version 1.0.0 corresponds to the November 19, 2026 analytical revision. The main replication archive derives from the accompanying private supplement v1.0. Each of the 1,602 original main request/response files, including failures, retains its original bytes. The archived training-response hashes, predictions and original prediction freeze identify the inputs used before the reserved choices were collected. The public protocol and configuration files preserve scientific settings. Administrative spending limits, account details, funding instructions and dispatch utilities are outside this distribution. The scenario input manifest records checksums of the public inputs. Its original checksum map is in `provenance/` inside the archive. The historical main plan manifest identifies original prespecified files and their hashes, including files used only for collection. SHA256SUMS in each ZIP covers the distributed files.

Development records derive from the seven stage archives held by the author. They preserve the scientific request payload, provider reply, response text or error, and stage-specific design and CSV records where available. Development records that appeared again as historical evidence in a later archive are included at their originating stage. The pretest also includes its separately identified accounting recheck where present. All development observations are separate from the 1,602 main attempts.

## Reading the choice data

One row in `data/choices.csv` is one first attempt. Identify an attempt by `model` and `episode_id`. The `index` follows the interleaved collection schedule across models. `mandate` is reference, cost or protection. The stage distinguishes fee controls, estimation, reserved prediction (holdout), and exact repeats. A `case_id` identifies a physical offer pair. An order of XY displays X as A and Y as B. YX reverses that mapping. `repeat_of` identifies an earlier presentation when the task is an exact repeat. Read the response status before using a choice: `physical_choice` codes X, Y or DECLINE, while `display_choice` codes A, B or DECLINE. A blank choice on a failed request is missing data.

The price is USD 1.00, the separate fixed reserve is USD 20.00, and reserve loss, if it occurs, is USD 10.00. The fee field in the profile data is in US dollars. Reserve-loss probability is a probability on the 0–1 scale. Recovery is the share of the $1 principal returned after seller non-delivery. The two fee forms use a linear term and an optional hinge at $0.020. The held-out offers consist of 16 physical pairs in both orders per condition. The pair is the resampling unit. Exact repeat tasks are excluded from training and prediction scoring.

In `matched.csv` and `specifications.csv`, recovery gains are percentage points conditional on purchase. Switching fees are in US cents. Censored boundaries must be read with their status. In `smoothing.csv`, a negative paired log-loss difference favors the fitted logit. Fee specifications and benchmark smoothing were reconsidered during revision. They are post-estimation checks.
